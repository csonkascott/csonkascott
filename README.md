## Orblo

**Your next good customer, with the reasons attached.**

A sales research app for founders who sell. Kindred finds a few companies worth your time from public, dated signals, shows you why with sources you can check, and drafts a note in your voice. You send it yourself.

**[▶ Try the clickable sample](https://csonkascott.github.io/kindred-demo/)** (two minutes, no sign up) · **[How it's built](https://github.com/csonkascott/kindred-demo#how-kindred-is-built)**

What I hold it to:

- **Every claim is cited or labelled.** A brief line links to the public page it came from, or it's marked inferred. What it can't find, it says so.
- **A person always sends.** Kindred never sends on its own. You copy the draft or send it from your own Gmail or Outlook.
- **A few good companies, not a list of thousands.** Each one comes with a dated reason to write now, and the ones it left out come with a reason too.

**Built with:** TypeScript · Next.js · React · Supabase (Postgres, row level security) · Stripe · Claude API · Playwright · Vitest · Vercel

The app is private and invite only. The sample uses made up data.
